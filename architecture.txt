Note the inverted $Effort$ metric—this ensures that short, impactful tasks mathematically score higher and clear the bottleneck first.  The mathematical architecture scales as:$$Score = (Priority \times 0.4) + (Urgency \times 0.4) + ((6 - Effort) \times 0.2)$$In practice, the codebase multiplies this result by $20$ and rounds it to supply a clean, readable $1\text{ to }100$ priority weight scale inside our view sorting routines.
